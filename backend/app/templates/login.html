Floor Balance · the operational control layer

Know what is eligible before the next stage starts.

Floor Balance is more than a report. It is the live operational balance used to understand available WIP by batch, product stage and location while processing teams move material through the plant.

01Fetch the right availability. Work from company, production-for and allowed-location context, with batch and product details kept close to the movement. 02Validate consumption before save. Eligible source balance checks help prevent insufficient input from being consumed at a processing stage. 03Keep corrections accountable. Edits and cancellations refresh or reverse related balance impact, preserving the WIP picture.

Current-day availability reflects live ERP transactions. Historical review uses the 09:00 IST daily snapshot, or the latest earlier snapshot where needed.
